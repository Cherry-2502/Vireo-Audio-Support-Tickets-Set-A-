The current export contains 11,875 deduplicated tickets from January 2025 through June 2026. Under a conservative proxy for the policy's same-issue rule, 980 tickets (8.25%) are potential repeats. The export has no issue ID, so this is a directional estimate that needs validation before it is treated as a confirmed repeat rate.

A 30% reduction would be about ₹12,700 per quarter. That reduction is a pilot scenario, not a demonstrated result; it depends on an intervention actually preventing contacts. At the blended ₹290 rate, the comparable scenario is about ₹14,260 per quarter.
